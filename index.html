🎮 Game of Life

My take on Conway's classic: three colors of cells, each with its own survival rules

  • Click place / remove a cell (while paused)
  • A S D yellow / red / blue cells
  • Space play / pause
  • ↑ ↓ faster / slower
  • R back to your starting pattern
  • Q clear the board

Built with C and raylib, compiled to WebAssembly · View source
